# Press-conference question research

The in-game catalogue uses original wording, informed by recurring editorial
themes in Romanian football coverage rather than copied article text.

## Sources reviewed

- GSP: pre-match coverage involving supporter expectations, European objectives,
  transfer activity, opposition analysis and possible career moves.
  - https://www.gsp.ro/fotbal/liga-1/costel-galca-conferinta-inainte-de-rapid-u-cluj-etapa-3-play-off-924440.html
  - https://www.gsp.ro/fotbal/liga-1/rapid-fcsb-marius-sumudica-782475.html
- Digi Sport: questions about slow starts, fear, defensive concentration, board
  support, dressing-room unity, resignation and pressure after poor results.
  - https://www.digisport.ro/fotbal/liga-1/ivaylo-petev-scandal-cu-un-reporter-la-conferinta-de-presa-atunci-nu-mi-ai-fi-pus-intrebarea-asta-2822499
  - https://www.digisport.ro/fotbal/liga-1/costel-galca-a-dat-cartile-pe-fata-dupa-5-meciuri-la-rand-fara-victorie-la-rapid-4233115
- Fanatik: questions about refereeing pressure, recruitment requests, squad depth,
  rotation decisions, loans and accountability for selection choices.
  - https://www.fanatik.ro/se-teme-adrian-mihalcea-de-arbitraj-la-uta-fcsb-raspunsul-ferm-al-antrenorului-exista-presiune-video-21393449
  - https://www.fanatik.ro/costel-galca-a-rabufnit-inainte-de-farul-rapid-intrebarea-care-l-a-deranjat-cum-m-am-plans-explica-mi-video-21384525

## Themes represented in the game

- tactics, formation, substitutions and slow starts;
- refereeing and controversial decisions;
- transfers, loans, thin squads and player contracts;
- board backing, owner pressure and job security;
- dressing-room confidence, unity and leadership;
- supporter expectations, boos and derby pressure;
- team selection, rotation, academy opportunities and criticised players;
- title, play-off, European qualification and other season objectives.

Each conference stores one deterministic question. This keeps saved games stable
and prevents the 255-character `topic` field from being overflowed by an entire
question catalogue.
